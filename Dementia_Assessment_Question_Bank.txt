Dementia Assessment Question Bank
LEVEL 1: NORMAL / VERY EARLY COGNITIVE CHANGE
1. What is today’s full date?
2. What day of the week is it?
3. What season are we in?
4. What city are we in right now?
5. What year did you last celebrate a major festival?
6. Subtract 7 from 100 once.
7. Spell the word WORLD backwards.
8. Repeat these numbers backward: 4 – 9 – 2
9. Say the months of the year in reverse order.
10. Name as many animals as you can in 60 seconds.
11. What do people use a key for?
12. How are a train and a bicycle similar?
13. Repeat: “No ifs, ands, or buts.”
14. Copy this simple geometric shape.
15. Draw a clock showing 10 minutes past 11.
LEVEL 2: MILD DEMENTIA
16. What month is it?
17. What place are we in right now?
18. Are we in a hospital, clinic, or home?
19. Remember these words: Apple – Chair – Coin
20. What were the three words I asked you to remember?
21. What did you eat for breakfast today?
22. Count backward from 20 to 1.
23. Subtract 3 from 20, then again.
24. Repeat these numbers: 6 – 1 – 8 – 4
25. What is this object called? (Pen / Watch)
26. Follow this instruction: “Close your eyes.”
27. Write a complete sentence.
LEVEL 3: MODERATE DEMENTIA
28. What time of day is it right now?
29. Do you know where you are?
30. Is it day or night?
31. Do you remember the name of this place?
32. What is your full name?
33. Who usually comes with you to the hospital or home?
34. Clap your hands two times.
35. Touch your nose, then your ear.
36. What is this used for? (Spoon / Cup)
37. Can you repeat this word: “House”?
38. Take the paper and place it on the table.
LEVEL 4: SEVERE DEMENTIA
39. Are you a man or woman?
40. Are you sitting or lying down?
41. Is it hot or cold right now?
42. Can you recognize this object?
43. Do you recognize this person?
44. Open your mouth.
45. Close your eyes.
46. Raise your hand.
47. Point to the door.

48. Are you comfortable right now?
49. Does this sound loud or soft?
50. Can you smile?
